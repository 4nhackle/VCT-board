Privacy policy

Effective 2026-11-08 · Contact biggest-cannula9b@icloud.com

This is a summary. The Korean original prevails. Read the Korean original (개인정보처리방침).

1. Visitor information

  • There is no sign-up or login. The site does not collect personal information such as names or contact details directly.
  • For visit statistics we use GoatCounter (goatcounter.com). It uses no cookies and stores no information that identifies visitors. It processes only aggregate data: page views, referral source, browser and device type, screen size and a rough country. No ads are shown.
  • Cloudflare Web Analytics is also used to measure visits and page performance: visit metrics, page load time and Core Web Vitals (page experience measures). Cloudflare states that it "does not use any client-side state, such as cookies or localStorage, to collect usage metrics" (cloudflare.com/web-analytics). Other processing and retention follow the Cloudflare privacy policy.
  • The site is served by GitHub Pages. GitHub may process access logs (such as IP addresses) to run the service; the site operator does not receive those logs.

2. Feedback feature

The feedback tab has two sending options.

  • Send button: the visitor's message, the optional source link and the chosen type are sent to the form service web3forms (api.web3forms.com), which forwards them to the operator's email. The feature does not ask for a name or email address. Connection data that web3forms processes, and its retention period, follow the web3forms privacy policy.
  • The feedback tab loads the bot check hCaptcha (Intuition Machines, Inc.) when it is opened, and Send requires a check first. hCaptcha processes IP addresses, browser type, operating system and device type, and verification data such as mouse movement, scrolling and key presses. It can set cookies. Cookie data may be kept for up to one year after the cookie expires. For details, see the hCaptcha privacy policy.
  • Mail app button: the visitor's own mail app opens and the visitor sends the mail. The site does not send this content. The Copy button only copies the text to the clipboard and sends it nowhere.

Received content is used only for replies and improvements and is deleted once handled. Do not put personal information in your message.

3. Players and coaches shown on the site

  • Names, teams, positions, nationality and transfer status confirmed by public reports and public sources. Real names, dates of birth and career details may be included.
  • Each item has a source link, and only facts confirmed in the source are shown. Full articles are not copied.
  • The person concerned or their representative may request correction or removal of wrong information. Tell us by email and we will handle it promptly after checking. Reports at the reporting stage are marked as not yet official, and they may change.

4. Retention and third parties

The site keeps no personal information about visitors. Apart from the visit statistics in section 1 (GoatCounter, Cloudflare Web Analytics) and the Send option in section 2 (via web3forms, with the hCaptcha check), visitor information is not given to third parties and is not used for ads. Retention for the services in sections 1 and 2 follows each service's own policy. Pressing a support link takes you to that service; it is not a transfer of data by this site.

5. Support

Support is handled by external services: Ko-fi, and KakaoPay (Korean domestic transfer, Korean page only). The site does not receive or store payment or supporter information. Pressing a support link takes you to that service, where its own privacy policy applies.

6. Changes to this policy

When processing changes, for example when ads are introduced or the analytics tool is replaced, this document is updated first and its effective date is revised.
